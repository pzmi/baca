### 2. Iter-4 candidates [per `doc/roadmap.md` Phase-2 "remaining"]

Empirical outcome says BC warm-start is valuable (+1.01 floor, first nonzero winrate) but PPO fine-tune as-tuned erases it. The cheapest probes first:

1. **Retune PPO warm-start** — shorter horizons (50k / 100k timesteps), tighter clip (0.05), lower `--initial-lr-scale` (0.1). Isolates whether length, step size, or architecture caused the regression. ~15 min per A/B run, can test 4-6 variants in under 2h. `checkpoints/v3-bc/bc_model.zip` is the baseline to beat.
2. **Return-weighted BC loss** — upsample the ~5% of BC games that win during training to bias imitation toward wins. No new data needed; ~30 min to implement + 10 min to retrain BC. Could plausibly add 0.5-2% winrate without any architecture change.
3. **Larger BC dataset** — 100-200k games at 29 g/s sustained ≈ 1-2 hours. BC loss plateaued at epoch 3 in iter-3, so diminishing returns possible, but worth one pass.
4. **LSTM / recurrent policy** — addresses partial observability (hidden deck, enemy intent two turns ahead). Needs BC re-collection with history windows. ~1 day of work.
5. **Wider MLP** — `features_dim=128`, `net_arch=[128, 128]`. Cheapest capacity lift; deferred because the 71.8% BC holdout accuracy plateau could be representation-bound and this might help.

### 3. Investigate why PPO regressed (diagnostic, not fix)

`doc/findings.md` "Iter-3 verdict" section ranks four hypotheses but we didn't conclusively isolate the root cause. Tensorboard logs at `checkpoints/tensorboard/MaskablePPO_3/` (and subsequent) preserve per-rollout `explained_variance`, `policy_gradient_loss`, `entropy_loss`, `approx_kl`. A post-hoc read of those could tell us:

- Did the critic destabilise early (EV crossing negative)? → supports hypothesis #2 (critic underfit).
- Did `approx_kl` climb above 0.03 in first 10k steps? → supports hypothesis #4 (hyperparameter miscalibration).
- Did mean reward dip at any point or monotonically stay at 0.05? → informs whether policy *moved* at all.

This is ~30 min of tensorboard-reading work and could retire two of the four hypotheses before iter-4 starts experimenting.

## Known caveats

- **BC-only at 1.20% on n=500 is below the Step 6 acceptance band [2.56%, 4.56%]** (which was based on ±1% of HeuristicBot's 3.56%). At n=200 Step 6 measured 2.0% — so the n=500 Step 9 rerun regressed from the smaller sample, consistent with ~1.3% 95%-CI half-width noise on n=200 vs ~0.8% on n=500. Not a bug; the true BC winrate sits around 1-2%, which is genuinely below the teacher.
- **PPO fine-tune ran only 3-4 minutes per 200k-timestep run**, vs plan's 15-25 min estimate. Throughput was ~900 timesteps/sec total = 113/sec per env on n_envs=8, in line with iter-2. The estimate was conservative. No issue.
- **SB3 `.learn()` stdout was quieter than iter-2 runs** — only EvalCallback messages visible, no per-rollout `explained_variance / policy_gradient_loss` table. Plan §5 Step 8 asked to check EV > 0 at 10k steps; couldn't do it in stdout. Tensorboard logs preserve the data if someone wants to audit.
- **`checkpoints/v3-bcppo/best/best_model.zip` and `checkpoints/v3-bcppo-valuewarm/best/best_model.zip` are early-training snapshots**, not end-of-training. SB3's `MaskableEvalCallback` `New best mean reward!` triggered by step 30k in both runs; mean reward then plateaued and the "best" was never updated. If iter-4 wants end-of-training policies, use `baca-latest.zip` in each dir.
- **BC dataset at `data/v3/` contains 50k NPZ files** (1.2 GB). R8 (plan) flagged that 50k files in one dir may hit filesystem quirks on some OSes; macOS APFS handles it fine but be aware if moving dataset to Linux/ext4.
- **Encoder is frozen per plan D7** — `CARD_IDS` append-only and `data/v3/CARD_IDS_snapshot.txt` is the trust boundary. `BcDataset(root)` raises `ValueError` if the snapshot drifts from `encoder.CARD_IDS`. If engine adds cards before iter-4, either append to `CARD_IDS` and regenerate the dataset, or pin the engine checkout.
- **PPO hyperparameter-override was a live bug** shipped as commit `c275a04` — any external code that calls `MaskablePPO.load` on a BC zip WITHOUT using `_load_bc_model` will silently train with `n_steps=8, ent_coef=0.0`. `eval_cli.py` doesn't hit this because it only `.predict`s, but any future training entrypoint other than `train.py` needs to replicate the pattern.
- **TaskList volatility**: tasks got auto-completed by implementer agents marking them done, and the team-lead re-setting them on verifier BLOCK. Don't trust `TaskList` for iter-3 history — rely on this handover and the git log.
